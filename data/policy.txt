1. ABOUT NEXORA TECHNOLOGIES
============================================================

Nexora Technologies Private Limited is a fictional technology company
headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

The company develops enterprise software, cloud infrastructure, artificial
intelligence systems, workflow automation products, analytics platforms,
developer tools, and cybersecurity solutions.

Nexora was established in 2018 by a group of engineers and product
professionals who wanted to build software that could reduce repetitive
business work.

The company initially operated as a small software consultancy and gradually
transitioned into a product-focused technology organization.

Nexora's major business areas include:

- Cloud platforms
- Artificial intelligence
- Enterprise automation
- Data engineering
- Cybersecurity
- Developer productivity
- Business analytics
- Customer experience software

As of October 2026, Nexora has approximately 850 employees.

Employees are distributed across:

Bengaluru
Hyderabad
Pune
Gurugram
Chennai
Remote locations across India

The company also works with customers located in North America, Europe,
Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East.

2. COMPANY MISSION
============================================================

Nexora's mission is:

"Build technology that makes complex work simple."

The company believes technology should reduce unnecessary complexity while
allowing people to focus on creative and meaningful work.

Nexora's long-term vision is to become a trusted provider of intelligent
software infrastructure for modern businesses.

3. COMPANY VALUES
============================================================

Nexora operates according to six primary values.

3.1 CUSTOMER FIRST

Employees should understand customer problems before proposing solutions.

Customer commitments should be treated seriously.

Employees must not make promises to customers that they are not authorized
to make.

3.2 OWNERSHIP

Employees are expected to take responsibility for their work.

Ownership includes:

- Meeting commitments
- Communicating blockers
- Taking responsibility for mistakes
- Following through on assigned work
- Asking for help when necessary

3.3 LEARN CONTINUOUSLY

Employees are encouraged to develop technical, professional, and leadership
skills.

Employees may participate in internal workshops, external courses,
conferences, mentoring programs, and certification programs.

3.4 RESPONSIBLE INNOVATION

Nexora encourages experimentation.

However, experimentation must respect:

- Security
- Privacy
- Customer confidentiality
- Intellectual property
- Legal requirements
- Internal approval processes

3.5 RESPECT

Employees must communicate respectfully regardless of role, seniority,
location, gender, background, or professional experience.

Disagreement is acceptable.

Disrespectful behavior is not.

3.6 TRANSPARENCY

Employees should communicate relevant information honestly.

Problems should be raised early rather than hidden until they become larger.

5. EMPLOYMENT CATEGORIES
============================================================

Nexora uses several employment categories.

FULL-TIME EMPLOYEE

A full-time employee normally works 40 hours per week and receives benefits
according to the applicable benefits plan.

PART-TIME EMPLOYEE

A part-time employee works fewer hours than the standard full-time schedule.

Benefits may differ according to the employment agreement.

FIXED-TERM EMPLOYEE

A fixed-term employee is hired for a defined period.

The employment agreement specifies the beginning and ending dates.

INTERN

Interns participate in structured learning and work programs.

Intern benefits are defined separately in the internship agreement.

CONSULTANT

Consultants provide professional services under a consulting agreement.

Consultants are generally not eligible for employee benefits unless
specifically stated in their agreement.

CONTRACTOR

Contractors provide services under a contract.

Contractors must follow applicable security, confidentiality, and workplace
requirements.

8. PROBATION
============================================================

The standard probation period for new full-time employees is six months.

During probation, managers assess:

- Job performance
- Technical ability
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Reliability
- Attendance
- Ownership
- Learning ability
- Compliance with company policies

A manager may recommend confirmation before the end of the probation period
when performance has been consistently strong.

Probation may be extended when additional evaluation is required.

An extension should normally be communicated in writing.

Probation extension does not automatically mean that an employee's
employment will be terminated.

11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST.

10. ATTENDANCE
============================================================

Employees are expected to attend work consistently.

Employees should inform their manager before the start of the workday if they
will be absent unexpectedly.

For planned leave, employees should use the HR system.

Repeated unexplained absence may result in corrective action.

Attendance requirements may differ for shift-based employees.

Employees must not intentionally record attendance on behalf of another
employee.

============================================================

Nexora uses a hybrid working model for eligible teams.

The standard hybrid arrangement requires employees to work from the office
at least two days per week.

Teams may establish additional office days when business requirements require
in-person collaboration.

Examples include:

- Product planning
- Customer workshops
- Team planning
- Security operations
- Hardware testing
- Company events

Employees should follow their team's approved hybrid schedule.


============================================================
12. REMOTE WORK
============================================================

Employees approved for remote work remain responsible for the same performance
and availability expectations as office-based employees.

Remote employees must:

- Maintain a secure workspace
- Protect confidential information
- Use company-approved devices
- Maintain reasonable internet connectivity
- Attend required meetings
- Follow security policies
- Remain reachable during agreed working hours

Employees should avoid working from public places when handling confidential
customer or company information.

============================================================

Nexora allows temporary work from another location under specific conditions.

Working from another country requires additional legal, tax, security, and
employment review.

Employees must not work internationally without written authorization.

14. LEAVE OVERVIEW
============================================================

The standard leave categories are:

Sick Leave: 12 days per year
Casual Leave: 6 days per year
Parental Leave: 15 working days
Bereavement Leave: up to 5 working days
Maternity Leave: according to applicable law
Adoption Leave: according to applicable policy

Leave entitlements may be prorated for employees joining during the year.


============================================================
15. ANNUAL LEAVE
============================================================

Employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least five working days in
advance.

Managers consider:

- Team workload
- Project deadlines
- Existing leave
- Customer commitments
- Employee needs

Managers should not unreasonably deny leave when adequate notice has been
provided and business requirements can be managed.

Up to 12 unused annual leave days may normally be carried into the next
calendar year.

Carry-forward rules are subject to applicable law and company policy.


============================================================
16. SICK LEAVE
============================================================

Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year.

Employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible.

For extended absence, People Operations may request appropriate medical
documentation where legally permitted.

Employees should not work while seriously ill when doing so would create
health, safety, or performance concerns.


============================================================
17. CASUAL LEAVE
============================================================

Employees receive six days of casual leave per year.

Casual leave may be used for:

- Personal appointments
- Family responsibilities
- Urgent personal matters
- Short-term emergencies
- Other reasonable personal needs

Employees should provide advance notice whenever practical.


============================================================
18. MATERNITY LEAVE
============================================================

Eligible employees receive maternity benefits in accordance with applicable
Indian law and company policy.

The standard policy provides up to 26 weeks of maternity leave for eligible
employees where applicable.

Employees should contact People Operations as early as reasonably possible
to plan maternity leave.

Managers must not discriminate against employees because they take legally
protected maternity leave.


============================================================
19. PATERNITY AND PARENTAL LEAVE
============================================================

Eligible employees may receive 15 working days of paid parental leave.

Parental leave may be used following:

- Birth of a child
- Adoption of a child
- Other qualifying parental events

Employees should normally use parental leave within three months of the
qualifying event.

Exceptions may be approved by People Operations.


============================================================
20. ADOPTION LEAVE
============================================================

Eligible employees adopting a child may receive paid adoption leave according
to company policy and applicable law.

Employees must provide required documentation to People Operations.

Adoption leave should normally be requested before the expected placement
date when possible.


============================================================
21. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
============================================================

Employees may receive up to five working days of paid bereavement leave after
the death of an immediate family member.

Immediate family may include:

- Parent
- Spouse
- Child
- Sibling

Additional leave may be approved depending on circumstances.

22. LEAVE WITHOUT PAY
============================================================

Unpaid leave may be approved when:

- A significant personal circumstance exists
- Extended absence is required

Unpaid leave may affect salary, benefits, and other employment calculations.

27. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLAN
============================================================

- Specific performance concerns

- Support provided
- Review period

A typical PIP lasts between 30 and 90 days.

A PIP does not automatically mean that employment will end.

Employees should receive an opportunity to understand expectations and improve.

============================================================
47. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE USAGE POLICY
============================================================

Nexora permits responsible use of approved artificial intelligence tools.

Employees must not paste confidential company information into public AI

Examples of information that should not be entered into unapproved AI tools:

- Customer personal information
- Production credentials
- Source code marked confidential
- Security vulnerabilities
- Internal financial information
- Employee personal information
- Unreleased product information

Employees may use approved AI tools for:

- Brainstorming
- Drafting
- Summarization
- Code assistance
- Documentation
- Data analysis

AI-generated output must be reviewed by a human before being used in
customer-facing or high-impact work.

Employees remain responsible for the accuracy of work produced with AI
assistance.

- People Operations
- Ethics Hotline
- Employee Relations Team

56. ANTI-DISCRIMINATION POLICY
============================================================

============================================================
57. PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT
============================================================

Nexora maintains a separate Prevention of Sexual Harassment policy.

Employees may report concerns through the Internal Committee.

The Internal Committee handles complaints according to applicable law and
company procedures.

Employees should not retaliate against a person who raises a complaint in
good faith.


============================================================
58. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
============================================================

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with their
responsibilities to Nexora.

Potential conflicts include:

- Working for a direct competitor
- Owning a significant financial interest in a vendor
- Hiring a close family member without disclosure
- Accepting inappropriate benefits from suppliers
- Using company opportunities for personal gain

Potential conflicts should be disclosed to the manager or People Operations.


============================================================
59. GIFTS AND ENTERTAINMENT
============================================================

Employees may receive modest business gifts when they do not influence
business decisions.

Employees must not accept gifts that create an expectation of favorable
treatment.

Cash gifts are generally prohibited.

Gifts valued above INR 5,000 require disclosure to Compliance.

Employees should decline gifts that may create an actual or perceived
conflict of interest.


============================================================
60. ANTI-BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION
============================================================

Nexora prohibits bribery and corruption.

Employees must not offer, promise, authorize, or accept improper payments
to influence business decisions.

This applies to dealings with:

- Customers
- Vendors
- Government officials
- Partners
- Consultants
- Agents

Suspected bribery should be reported immediately to Legal or Compliance.

Employees may report suspected serious misconduct.

Reports may involve:

- Fraud
- Bribery
- Financial misconduct
- Serious safety concerns
- Security violations
- Retaliation
- Serious policy violations

Reports can be submitted through the Ethics Hotline or Compliance Team.

63. DISCIPLINARY POLICY
============================================================

Policy violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

Possible actions include:

- Coaching
- Written warning
- Final warning
- Suspension where permitted
- Termination of employment

The response depends on factors including:

- Nature of the conduct
- Severity
- Intent
- Previous history
- Business impact
- Applicable law


============================================================
64. INVESTIGATION PROCESS
============================================================

When a serious concern is reported, Nexora may conduct an investigation.

An investigation may include:

1. Receiving the complaint
2. Initial assessment
3. Identifying relevant documents
4. Interviewing relevant individuals
5. Reviewing system records
6. Evaluating evidence
7. Preparing findings
8. Determining appropriate action

Investigations should be handled as confidentially as practical.

Confidentiality does not guarantee that information will never be disclosed.


============================================================
65. RETALIATION
============================================================

Retaliation against employees who make good-faith reports or participate in
investigations is prohibited.

Retaliation may include:

- Threats
- Unjustified punishment
- Harassment
- Intentionally reducing responsibilities as punishment
- Blocking legitimate opportunities because of a report

Employees who believe they are experiencing retaliation should contact
Employee Relations or the Ethics Team.


============================================================
66. GRIEVANCE PROCESS
============================================================

Employees who have workplace concerns should first consider discussing the
matter with their manager when appropriate.

If the issue cannot be resolved, employees may contact People Operations.

A formal grievance may include:

- Description of the issue
- Relevant dates
- Individuals involved
- Supporting documents
- Requested resolution

People Operations may investigate and provide an appropriate response.


============================================================
67. MANAGER RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

Managers are expected to:

- Set clear expectations
- Provide feedback
- Support employee development
- Approve leave fairly
- Maintain confidentiality
- Address workplace concerns
- Escalate serious issues
- Avoid retaliation
- Apply policies consistently

Managers should not promise specific disciplinary outcomes before an
investigation is completed.


============================================================
68. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
============================================================

Employees are responsible for:

- Performing assigned work
- Following policies
- Protecting company information
- Maintaining professional behavior
- Reporting concerns
- Completing required training
- Maintaining accurate employee records
- Taking care of company property


============================================================
69. OFFICE SAFETY
============================================================

Employees should follow office safety requirements.

Employees should report:

- Unsafe conditions
- Injuries
- Fire hazards
- Electrical hazards
- Security incidents

Emergency exits must remain unobstructed.


============================================================
70. EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
============================================================

In case of emergency, employees should prioritize personal safety.

Emergency situations may include:

- Fire
- Natural disaster
- Medical emergency
- Security threat
- Building evacuation

Employees should follow instructions from building security and emergency
personnel.


============================================================
71. DRUG AND ALCOHOL POLICY
============================================================

Employees must not report to work impaired by illegal drugs or alcohol.

Alcohol may only be consumed at company events where permitted by law and
company event rules.

Employees are expected to behave professionally at company events.


============================================================
72. DRESS AND PROFESSIONAL APPEARANCE
============================================================

Nexora generally follows a smart-casual workplace environment.

Employees should dress appropriately for:

- Customer meetings
- Interviews
- Presentations
- Formal events
- Office requirements

Certain roles may have additional safety or appearance requirements.


============================================================
73. WORKPLACE VISITORS
============================================================

Visitors must follow building security requirements.

Visitors may need to:

- Register at reception
- Present identification
- Wear a visitor badge
- Remain accompanied in restricted areas

Visitors must not access confidential systems.


============================================================
74. SECURITY ACCESS CARDS
============================================================

Employees must use their own access card.

Employees must not:

- Share access cards
- Allow unauthorized tailgating
- Copy access cards
- Use another employee's card

Lost access cards should be reported to Workplace Services immediately.


============================================================
75. PARKING AND TRANSPORTATION
============================================================

Parking availability varies by office.

Parking spaces may be assigned according to office rules.

Employees must follow building parking requirements.

Nexora is not responsible for unauthorized parking fines unless otherwise
stated in a specific office policy.


============================================================
76. REMOTE WORKSPACE SECURITY
============================================================

Remote employees should:

- Use private Wi-Fi where possible
- Avoid public computers
- Use company VPN when required
- Lock devices
- Avoid discussing confidential information in public
- Store documents only in approved systems

Employees should not allow family members or friends to use company laptops.


============================================================
77. TRAVEL SECURITY
============================================================

Employees traveling for business should follow company travel security
guidance.

Employees should protect:

- Company devices
- Travel documents
- Confidential information
- Credentials

International travel may require additional security review.


============================================================
78. CUSTOMER INTERACTION
============================================================

Employees interacting with customers represent Nexora.

Employees should:

- Communicate professionally
- Avoid unauthorized commitments
- Protect customer information
- Escalate serious complaints
- Document important decisions

Employees should not disclose internal company information to customers
unless authorized.


============================================================
79. MEDIA AND PUBLIC STATEMENTS
============================================================

Only authorized employees may speak on behalf of Nexora to the media.

Media inquiries should be directed to the Communications Team.

Employees may express personal opinions but should not present themselves as
official Nexora representatives without authorization.


============================================================
80. COMPANY EVENTS
============================================================

Nexora may organize:

- Team events
- Annual celebrations
- Offsites
- Conferences
- Workshops
- Hackathons
- Sports activities

Employees are expected to follow the Code of Conduct at company events.

Company events are considered workplace environments for purposes of relevant
company policies.


============================================================
81. EMPLOYEE RECOGNITION
============================================================

Nexora recognizes employees through programs such as:

- Peer recognition
- Customer appreciation
- Innovation awards
- Leadership awards
- Long-service recognition

Recognition programs may change annually.


============================================================
82. EMPLOYEE REFERRAL PROGRAM
============================================================

Employees may refer candidates for open positions.

Referral bonuses may be available for selected roles.

The referring employee must disclose any personal relationship with the
candidate where appropriate.

Employees involved in hiring decisions should not receive referral bonuses
for candidates they directly evaluate unless specifically approved.


============================================================
83. INTERNSHIP PROGRAM
============================================================

Nexora offers internships for students and early-career professionals.

Internships generally last between 8 and 24 weeks.

Interns may receive:

- Stipend
- Mentor support
- Training
- Project experience
- Certificate of completion

Interns must follow company security and confidentiality policies.


============================================================
84. CONTRACTOR POLICY
============================================================

Contractors must comply with relevant:

- Security requirements
- Confidentiality requirements
- Workplace policies
- Customer requirements

Contractors may only access systems required for their work.

Access should be removed when the contract ends.


============================================================
85. SEPARATION FROM EMPLOYMENT
============================================================

Employment may end through:

- Resignation
- Mutual separation
- Retirement
- End of fixed-term contract
- Termination
- Other legally recognized circumstances

Employees must complete the applicable exit process.


============================================================
86. RESIGNATION
============================================================

Employees who resign should submit a written resignation through the HR
system or according to their employment agreement.

The resignation should include the intended last working day.

The manager and People Operations will confirm the applicable notice period.


============================================================
87. NOTICE PERIOD
============================================================

The standard notice period for full-time employees is 60 calendar days unless
the employment agreement specifies otherwise.

Some senior roles may have longer notice periods.

The company may approve an earlier release.

Early release is not automatic and may depend on:

- Business requirements
- Handover completion
- Replacement availability
- Manager approval


============================================================
88. EXIT PROCESS
============================================================

The exit process normally includes:

1. Resignation submission
2. Notice period confirmation
3. Knowledge transfer
4. Asset return
5. Access removal
6. Exit interview
7. Final payroll processing
8. Benefits closure
9. Employment documentation

Employees should complete all required handover activities.


============================================================
89. FINAL SETTLEMENT
============================================================

Final settlement may include:

- Salary through last working day
- Eligible leave payout
- Approved expense reimbursement
- Applicable deductions
- Other contractual payments

The exact timing depends on payroll processing and completion of required
exit documentation.


============================================================
90. RETURN OF COMPANY PROPERTY
============================================================

Employees leaving Nexora must return company property.

This may include:

- Laptop
- Monitor
- Security key
- Access card
- Company phone
- Documents
- Other equipment

Failure to return company property may delay completion of the exit process
where legally permitted.


============================================================
91. EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION
============================================================

Employment verification requests should be sent to the People Operations
verification team.

The company generally confirms employment information according to its
verification procedure.

Managers should not independently provide unofficial employment verification
on behalf of Nexora.


============================================================
92. REHIRE POLICY
============================================================

Former employees may be considered for rehire depending on:

- Previous performance
- Reason for departure
- Conduct history
- Business needs
- Current role requirements

Employees who left while under serious disciplinary investigation may be
subject to additional review.


============================================================
93. POLICY EXCEPTIONS
============================================================

Policy exceptions require appropriate approval.

Employees should not assume that a manager can override every People
Operations policy.

Depending on the policy, approval may be required from:

- Manager
- Department Head
- People Operations
- Legal
- Finance
- Security
- Executive Leadership

Exceptions should normally be documented.


============================================================
94. POLICY CHANGES
============================================================

Nexora may update policies when business, legal, regulatory, or operational
requirements change.

Employees will be notified of material policy changes through approved
internal communication channels.

The latest approved policy version takes precedence over older versions.


============================================================
95. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
============================================================

QUESTION:
How many annual leave days do I receive?

ANSWER:
Full-time employees normally receive 24 paid annual leave days per calendar
year.

QUESTION:
How many sick leave days do I receive?

ANSWER:
Employees normally receive 12 paid sick leave days per calendar year.

QUESTION:
Can I work from home?

ANSWER:
Eligible employees may work remotely according to their team's approved
hybrid arrangement.

QUESTION:
How many days must I work from the office?

ANSWER:
The standard hybrid policy requires at least two office days per week for
eligible hybrid employees. Individual teams may have additional requirements.

QUESTION:
Can I work from another city?

ANSWER:
Temporary work from another Indian city may be permitted for up to 30
calendar days per year with manager approval.

QUESTION:
Can I work from another country?

ANSWER:
International remote work requires prior written authorization because tax,
legal, employment, security, and immigration requirements may apply.

QUESTION:
What is the notice period?

ANSWER:
The standard notice period is 60 calendar days unless the employee's
employment agreement specifies a different period.

QUESTION:
How much health insurance do I receive?

ANSWER:
The standard company-sponsored health insurance coverage is INR 5,00,000
per policy year for eligible employees.

QUESTION:
Can I claim internet expenses?

ANSWER:
Eligible remote employees may claim up to INR 1,500 per month subject to
documentation and applicable policy requirements.

QUESTION:
What is the annual learning allowance?

ANSWER:
Eligible employees receive an annual learning allowance of INR 25,000.

QUESTION:
Can I use ChatGPT or another AI tool for work?

ANSWER:
Employees may use approved AI tools according to the AI Usage Policy.
Confidential, personal, customer, security-sensitive, or restricted
information must not be entered into unapproved public AI systems.

QUESTION:
Who do I contact if I experience harassment?

ANSWER:
Employees may contact People Operations, Employee Relations, the Internal
Committee where applicable, or the Ethics Hotline.

QUESTION:
Can I report a concern anonymously?

ANSWER:
Certain concerns may be submitted through the Ethics Hotline using the
available anonymous reporting mechanism.

QUESTION:
What happens if I violate company policy?

ANSWER:
The company may take corrective or disciplinary action depending on the
nature and severity of the violation.

QUESTION:
Can I apply for another internal position?

ANSWER:
Eligible employees may apply for internal positions. Employees generally
should have completed at least six months in their current role unless an
exception is approved.

QUESTION:
How long does probation last?

ANSWER:
The standard probation period is six months.

QUESTION:
Can probation be extended?

ANSWER:
Yes. Probation may be extended when additional evaluation is required.

QUESTION:
Can I take leave without approval?

ANSWER:
Employees should obtain approval before planned leave. Emergency situations
should be communicated to the manager as soon as reasonably possible.

QUESTION:
What happens to unused annual leave?

ANSWER:
Up to 12 unused annual leave days may normally be carried into the following
calendar year, subject to applicable rules.

QUESTION:
Who should I contact if my salary is incorrect?

ANSWER:
Employees should contact Payroll or People Operations through the HR support
portal.

QUESTION:
What should I do if my company laptop is stolen?

ANSWER:
Immediately report the incident to IT and Security Operations and provide
the required details.

QUESTION:
Can I install software on my company laptop?

ANSWER:
Only approved or authorized software should be installed on company devices.

QUESTION:
Can I share my company password with IT?

ANSWER:
No. Employees must never share passwords. IT should use approved support
procedures instead.

QUESTION:
Can I use company data with an AI tool?

ANSWER:
Only approved AI tools may be used for company data, and employees must
follow data classification requirements.

QUESTION:
Can I accept a gift from a vendor?

ANSWER:
Modest gifts may be acceptable when they do not influence business decisions.
Gifts above INR 5,000 must be disclosed to Compliance.

QUESTION:
Can I work for another company while working at Nexora?

ANSWER:
Employees must disclose potential conflicts of interest. Outside employment
that conflicts with Nexora responsibilities or interests may not be permitted.

QUESTION:
Who can speak to the media?

ANSWER:
Only authorized company representatives may speak on behalf of Nexora.

97. GLOSSARY
============================================================

ANNUAL LEAVE

Paid time off normally used for vacation or personal purposes.

PROBATION

An initial employment period during which performance and suitability are
evaluated.

PIP

Performance Improvement Plan.

People Operations

The function responsible for employee experience, HR processes, policies,
benefits, and employee relations.

MANAGER

The person responsible for an employee's day-to-day management.

CORE HOURS

The standard period during which employees are expected to be available for
collaboration.

CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Information that is not publicly available and requires protection.

PERSONAL DATA

Information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication.

EAP

Employee Assistance Program.

INTERNAL COMMITTEE

The committee responsible for handling applicable workplace sexual harassment
complaints according to relevant law and company procedures.

NOTICE PERIOD

The period between resignation and the employee's final working day.

FINAL SETTLEMENT

The process of calculating and paying eligible amounts when employment ends.

WHISTLEBLOWER

A person who reports suspected misconduct or wrongdoing.

RETALIATION

Adverse treatment directed at a person because they raised a concern or
participated in an investigation.
